Put your reflexes to the test in a quick, easy-to-play reaction game.

Tap to begin a round, then wait for the signal to turn green. Tap as soon as it does to see your reaction time in milliseconds. Tap too early and the round is marked as a false start—try again.

Reflex Arcade shows your latest result and your best result for the current session, so you can challenge yourself round after round. The rules are simple and each attempt is quick.

How to play:
• Tap “Tap to Start.”
• Wait for the green signal.
• Tap as quickly as you can.
• Check your latest time and session best, then play again.

Take a short break, come back, and see how quickly you can react.
