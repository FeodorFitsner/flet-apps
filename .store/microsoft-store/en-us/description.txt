Reflex Arcade is a quick reaction-time game to challenge your reflexes.

Tap the play area to start a round. Wait for the signal to turn green, then tap as quickly as you can. Your reaction time appears in milliseconds. Tap too soon and it’s a false start—try again in a new round.

Your latest result and best result for the current session stay on screen, so you can compare attempts as you play. The session best resets when you close the app.

How to play:
1. Tap the play area to begin.
2. Wait for the green signal.
3. Tap as soon as it appears.
4. Check your latest time and session best.
5. Start another round and try to improve your time.

Choose a light or dark display theme with the toggle in the top-right corner. Reflex Arcade keeps the rules simple and each round quick.
