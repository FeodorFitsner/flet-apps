Reflex Arcade turns a simple signal into a quick test of timing.

Tap the play area to start a round. Wait through the short, randomized pause, then tap as soon as the panel turns green. Your reaction time appears in milliseconds.

Tap before the green signal and it’s a false start. Try another round and see if you can react faster.

Each session shows your latest result and your best time so far. The session best is temporary and resets when you close the app.

Choose light or dark mode with the switch in the top-right corner.

How to play
1. Tap to start a round.
2. Wait for the green signal.
3. Tap as quickly as you can.
4. Check your latest time and session best.
5. Play another round.

Simple controls, quick rounds, and clear results make Reflex Arcade easy to pick up whenever you have a moment.
